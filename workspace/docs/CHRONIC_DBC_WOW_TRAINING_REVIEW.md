### B. Final DBC v8 workflow (`DBC_V8_BATTLE_PLAN.md` lines 49–86)
- Script → approved/appropriate VO → measure actual sentence and word boundaries → visual beat table → sound map → render → final-encode visual/audio/subtitle checks.
- Word-complete: no scene/beat cut before the relevant spoken phrase is finished. Slower beats and readable evidence can coexist with overall pace.
- Real archive video first for things like aircraft/search; plausible labelled AI when no authentic image exists; code is animation/UI/map/annotation, not a crude fake hero object.
- Caption safe zone: don't composite footage on top of subtitles; check labels inside frame, text width and legibility. Verify all planned overlays actually render; no duplicated beats or repeated stock by accident.
- QC: sample final MP4 contact sheets, inspect suspect frames full resolution, check decode/frame count/black frames, proper audio and SRT. Don't claim an audio ear-check or perfect sync when it was not done.
- English Chronic long-form voice-01 is a channel-specific default; it does not override RAAZVIK's user-selected voice-39.
