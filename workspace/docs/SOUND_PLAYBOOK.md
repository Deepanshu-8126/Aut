## E) TIMING RULES (sabse important — training ka nichod)
1. **Sting punchline ke 0.1-0.15s BAAD** — VO ka aakhri word khatam, phir sting. Overlap = dono mar jaate hain.
2. **SILENCE WEAPON:** bade punchline se 0.3-0.5s pehle bed duck/cut. Silence → punch → sting. (NYT ka #1 trick)
3. Ek beat = EK sting. Chain sirf physical comedy me (girna = whistle→splat→boom, par har ek apne frame pe).
4. Bed switch HARD CUT scene-cut pe; fade sirf mood-melt (roast-to-love) pe.
5. Har 20-40s me kuch na kuch audio event ho (sting/switch/foley) — warna flat.
6. Trim longform sounds: dun_dun_dun→2s, sad_violin→2s, crickets→1.5s, anime_wow→1.2s, dhol→2s.
7. Micro-fades 0.02s HAR sfx pe (43s-bug lesson). Peak check har mix pe.

## 🎓 TOP-CREATOR SOUND TRAINING (Oct 28) — algorithm-grade audio, SAB CHANNELS
_Source: MrBeast retention analysis + creator sound-palette studies. Ye patterns ab hamara default hai._

### 1. AUDIO = RETENTION ENGINE (visuals sirf aadha game)
- **Har visual change pe audio cue** — text pop = "pop/tick", zoom = bass riser/drop, cut = whoosh. Bina sound ke visual change "flat" lagta hai, brain register nahi karta.
- **Pattern interrupt every 2-4s** (shorts: 1-3s): visual YA audio stimulus badlo — brain ka orientation reflex hijack hota hai, swipe karna physically mushkil.

### 2. THE REVEAL STACK (locked formula — MrBeast choreography)
`RISER (2-3s build) → HARD CUT riser → IMPACT HIT (moment of truth) → WHOOSH (next scene)`
- Montage/chaos = glitch FX. Reaction = heavy impact. Hamne rl04 mein yahi kiya (riser 10.60 → impact+slap 10.80) ✓ — ab HAR reveal pe default.

### 3. SILENCE = SABSE BADA PATTERN INTERRUPT
- Tense moment pe music FULL CUT → drone ya pura sannata → brain turant notice karta hai → anticipation vacuum → phir LOUD payoff. (Hamara "silence weapon" law = industry-proven.)

### 4. MUSIC = NARRATIVE METRONOME
- Rules/setup = upbeat staccato; decision/tension = music OUT; payoff = full energy wapas. Music EMOTION ke saath badle, timeline ke saath nahi.
- Background duck: VO/SFX cue ke neeche auto-duck, pauses mein wapas upar.

### 5. dB DISCIPLINE (engineer levels)
- Whoosh/cut SFX: −18 to −22dB (complement, overwhelm nahi)
- Impact/reveal: top layer, master peak ≤−1dB
- Pattern: SFX every 3-5s minimum energy content mein
