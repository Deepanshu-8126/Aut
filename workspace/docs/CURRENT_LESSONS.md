# Current lessons — practical anti-repeat checklist

Updated 30 October 2026. These supplement, not replace, the original manuals.

## User intent and communication
- Confirm the subject and platform before building. A reference video means style/technique unless the user explicitly asks for its subject. Spine was a reference for movement and delivery, not a topic to repeat.
- Act on feedback; do not keep asking the user to explain what they already specified. A user rejection overrides a machine SHIP verdict.
- Never promise a perfect or viral score. Do not claim eye/ear approval, a backup, a full pipeline pass, upload, identity of a voice or exactness of an edit without evidence.
- User wants a short upload kit, not a long internal-document dump.

## Voice / sync
- Funcyous current recorded voice lock: Kokoro `am_adam` (Oct 29); `am_puck` and `am_michael` rejected. FACTO `am_adam` notes in SOUND_PLAYBOOK override earlier voice lock entries. The exact voice ID used in the uploaded spine video has not been independently confirmed; don't label a new voice as identical.
- A continuous take or naturally connected speech is preferable to staccato disconnected line generation. Cap excessive pauses while preserving reveal beats and intelligibility; user disliked overly slow, stop-start Chimera VO. User's ear is final.
- Measure speech boundaries. Retiming VO requires matching scene changes, captions, SFX, and SRT; verify final-encode word-sync sheet. Automated transcription may mishear initial words or chromosome names: check XX/XY pronunciation and the actual audio.
- Original SOUND_MAP before build; source clean and SFX library first. Avoid stacking changes onto a finished mix when stems exist; keep original and use full-graph remix. Honor Chronic no-braam and channel-specific music.

## Visual and story
- User rejected Chimera versions even after high machine scores: Ken Burns, particles and color glows alone are not the spine reference's character-level life. Analyze actual reference and build story-relevant movement, not random effects just to boost a score.
- Spine style devices included controlled pose changes/image blends, breathing, blink, matched motion to VO, and mechanism visualizations. Recreate the technique, not its topic or copyrighted characters. No bone rig for ROop characters.
- Hook frame 1 must show clear stakes, and the promise must be paid off honestly. The swipe gate is only an internal heuristic, not a claim about YouTube's algorithm or audience behavior.
- AI image variants can change identity, pose and anatomy despite prompts; inspect side by side before blending. Reject mismatched keyframes, not ghostly morphs. Frame checks alone cannot prove animation quality.
- Real images must be correctly labeled representative; never imply another specimen is the actual subject. Verify facts, attribution links, license terms and crop/edits; no invented identity or untested medical claim. In Chimera, blood cells carried the twin's genetic material; other tested tissues were XX. Avoid stating every cell was tested.
- Caption first frame, thumbnail/title pair, contain-fit, image-source transform math for tracked circles, and no clipped text. Inspect final encode in full-res and contact sheets, especially transitions and loop seam.

## Storage and backups
- On 30 Oct the user confirmed removal of ALL nine local output MP4/MOV/MKV/WEBM files, including holds and originals. Supporting sources, docs, SRT and thumbnails were kept. Never assume a local final still exists.
- Project deletion is consequential; ask for explicit scope before deleting protected docs, masters, thumbnails or audio. Verify backup availability if recovery is promised; local docs inventory/checksums are not backups.
- Snapshots exclude caches, dependencies and `/var/tmp`; only workspace-root files are durable subject to snapshot cap. Avoid relying on ephemeral frames or audio.
