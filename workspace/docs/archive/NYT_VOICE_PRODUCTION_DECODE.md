## 1) VOICE STYLE — NARRATION DNA (transcript se nikala)

### 1.1 POV + Tone
- **First-person memoir RANT**, present-tense, seedha viewer se baat ("तुम", "तुम्हें").
- Tone = dost ke saath baithe ho aur wo apni पीड़ा sunaa raha hai — **complaint comedy**, self-deprecating victim ("फॉरेनरों जैसा स्कैम हो रहा हूं मैं").
- **Tezz conversational Hindi + English nouns ghused ke** ("टूरिस्ट वाले एक्सपीरियंस", "आई एम अनसब्सक्राइबिंग फॉर नाउ", "टेक्निकली").

### 1.2 Tika kalaam (verbal tics) — frequency order
| Tic | Use |
|---|---|
| **"भाई"** | har segment-start pe, emphasis pe (सबसे zyada) |
| **"अरे"** | reaction/objection start |
| **"यार"** | emotional sigh |
| **"खैर, बैक टू [topic]"** | tangent se wapas aane ka hinge |
| **"एक तो..."** | nayi complaint ka opener |
| **"बाय द वे"** | trivia drop |
| **"साला/बेचारा"** | character pe affection-roast |

### 1.3 COMEDY ENGINES (7 — writing checklist)
1. **EXAGGERATION LADDER:** सच → बड़ा → absurd। ("भीड़ इतनी... ek banda train ke NEECHE kood gaya — हां, उसे वहां KAM chotein aayi.")
2. **ABSURD-LOGIC TWIST:** fake-news setup ko logic se ulta ghuma do ("25 मौतें = ek hi banda 25 baar jhatka kha ke zinda ho raha tha — तो टेक्निकली...").
3. **SKIT INSERT:** narration ruk jaati hai, 2-6 line ka ACTED scene chalta hai (alag character voices), phir narration resume. Har segment me minimum 1 skit. (= Almost Real ka "inserted acted scene" pattern bhi yahi hai.)
4. **REGIONAL ACCENT CHARACTERS:** Bengali uncle ("কি কথা হচ্ছে"), Bihari/MP dost phone pe ("ले रे", "हट, नहीं खाना, इज्जत ही नहीं भाई की"), Punjabi paaji. Accent HI character hai.
5. **CALLBACK:** ek joke植 karo, 2-3 segment baad wapas lao ("चरित्रहीन गली" 3 baar; uncle ka theme song "ढंग ढंग ढंग" 2 baar).
6. **WORDPLAY/LITERALISM:** "बेक्ड रसगुल्ला? ये रसगुल्ला थोड़ी ना है, ये तो बस **गुल्ला** है"; "थम्स अप पुचका" → "मैंने थम्स **डाउN** करके घुसा दिया".
7. **META-COMEDY:** apne channel/comments/algorithm pe jokes ("We want old NYT" comments ka roast; "मुझे नहीं पता ये video kaise perform karegi, प्लीज मुझे बचा लेना").

### 1.4 STRUCTURE (Kolkata Is Bad ka skeleton — reusable)
1. **COLD OPEN (0-15s):** shock statement + PRANK SKIT ("मैंने घर छोड़ दिया... पापा जी वो सलोनी... प्रैंक प्रैंक पापा जी!") — pehle 15 sec me hi ek pura mini-joke.
2. **CONTEXT + META BLOCK:** kyun aaya, audience se direct baat, channel-meta joke.
3. **4-7 OBSERVATION SEGMENTS:** har ek = complaint topic (language → scam → dost ke call → mausam → galiyaan → sweets → local train → Durga Puja). Har segment: rant → skit → punchline → next.
4. **ROAST-TO-LOVE FLIP (end se pehle):** "लेकिन मैं कुछ भी बोलूं — कोलकाता जितनी कलरफुल सिटी... कहीं नहीं है." ❤️ (Hamara parody "affectionate" rule == yahi.)
5. **CTA (desperate-playful + SPECIFIC NUMBER + REASON):** "मुझे इस वीडियो पे **25,000 कमेंट** चाहिए अगर तुम्हें अगली वीडियो जल्द चाहिए... बचा लेना."
6. **OUTRO GAG:** story world CTA ke baad bhi ghus aati hai (Bengali log ghar aa gaye + Bengali song me "Not Your Type ke support koro, subscribe koro").

### 1.5 DELIVERY (audio)
- Fast, natural, **flowing** — sentences commas se behte hain, exclamation-chains nahi (hamara VOICE LAW match karta hai ✅).
- Punchline pe pitch/energy spike, uske turant baad flat deadpan ("हां, उसे वहां कम चोटें आईं." — deadpan hi joke hai).
- Character voices = same narrator ka pitch/accent shift + thoda processing; har character ki ALAG rhythm (Bengali slow-sing-song, Bihari loud-flat, Papa ji calm).
- Formality flip as joke: gali-mohalla tone ke beech achanak shuddh formal ("माफी चाहूंगा भाई साहब, तहे दिल से माफी चाहूंगा").

## 2) PRODUCTION DECODE (video kaise bante hain)
1. **SCRIPT FIRST, art baad me** — Ayu script+voice pehle; Aryan visuals us par. (Hamara LIVING POSTER "story FIRST" se match.)
2. **Storyboard → color palette ek separate step hai** — har video ki apni mood palette (Kolkata = yellow taxi/teal; school = pastel). Isliye unke frames itne cohesive lagte hain.
3. **Sound design = script ke saath seamless align** — SFX sting har punchline pe (Almost Real decode se match), theme-song gags ("ढंग ढंग ढंग" announcement) tak sound hi joke hai.
4. **Dono bhai script discussion karte hain:** jokes, character dev, scene transitions, "do stories ka interconnection" — fir hi final.
5. **Refine pass:** jo final me "sit" nahi karta use re-do karte hain + sponsor changes.
6. **No deadline pressure on length/quantity** — 1/week max, 8-min avg, 26.5M avg views. **Patience = moat.**
