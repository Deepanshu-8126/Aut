## हर chapter की 8 layers
1. **Narrative:** approved master का exact uncut time range, ear-verified transcript, fact and uncertainty notes।
2. **Picture:** correctly identified original people/place/object/document; modern illustrative shot only as illustration।
3. **Motion:** purposeful crop, map, annotation या animation—fake character motion, continuous shakes और repeated holds नहीं।
4. **Evidence:** citation, date, source context, rights status; readable document highlights।
5. **Text:** Devanagari/Hinglish short labels, consistent hierarchy, safe margins; no permanent black bars।
6. **Sound:** chapter sound map BEFORE mix; relevant SFX, appropriate music rights, no double layers; original VO clear।
7. **Captions:** Hindi SRT from human-checked transcript; word and caption timings verified against listening।
8. **QC/approval:** shot-level semantic + visual match, human watch/listen, source/rights and factual review; a machine decode alone is not editorial approval।

## Chapter-by-chapter workflow
1. Master VO और एक chapter का transcript recover; waveform सुनें, बिना cut के absolute boundaries तय करें।
2. नीचे के ASR को सुनकर सही script करें; हर लाइन के आगे visual, source, on-screen text और sound का shot sheet भरें।
3. Assets shortlist एवं rights review; missing real visual को सच-सच mark करें, approved alternative तय करें।
4. Sound map और visual storyboard; फिर chapter बनाएं। पहले short representative style/sync test user से approve।
5. Chapter का picture, sound, captions व sources एक साथ QC; approved version lock. बिना approval अगले chapter को final न मानें।
6. सभी approved chapters join: Kaggle पर 1920×1080 H.264 master, constant 24 fps, audio 48 kHz; exact frame/sample boundaries verify, master VO intact। Source quality below HD को upscaled HD कहें, native HD नहीं।
7. Full watch/listen, whole-film captions+credits+source manifest, black/freeze/decode tests, timeline and voice continuity check; output only after all gates pass. No guarantee of zero errors; human review essential।
