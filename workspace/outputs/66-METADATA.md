## TITLE
Why You Can NEVER Unboil an Egg 🥚🔥

## DESCRIPTION
Drop an egg into boiling water... and you've started a machine that can never be switched off. 🥚

Inside the white: millions of coiled protein springs, floating free — that's why raw egg pours like liquid. At 63°C the heat shakes them until they unravel. And unraveled springs tangle. By 82°C they lock into one solid mesh, trapping the water inside. Liquid becomes rubber.

Fry it, you can scrape it. Burn it, you can bin it. But a boiled egg can never be unboiled. The springs never let go.
