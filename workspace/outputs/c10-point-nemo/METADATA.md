DESC:
In 1997, deep-sea hydrophones across the South Pacific recorded an ultra-low frequency sound louder than any known creature on Earth. The epicenter was near Point Nemo — the Oceanic Pole of Inaccessibility, thousands of miles from the nearest human.

Scientists called it The Bloop. Official reports later linked it to deep Antarctic icequakes, but the raw recordings continue to puzzle researchers worldwide.
