Your dishwasher doesn't fill up like a bathtub. So how does every plate get clean? At the bottom, a small pool of water is heated and mixed with detergent. A pump forces that water into hollow spray arms. Tiny angled jets shoot out, making the arms spin like a sprinkler. The jets blast the dishes. Water falls back down through a filter, then gets pumped around again. At the end of the wash, the dirty water drains, and fresh water rinses everything. All that cleaning, without a full tub.
