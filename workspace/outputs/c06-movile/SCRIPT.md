# C06 — MOVILE CAVE (Chronic, Dossier Mode)
Target 30-34s | voice-30 | EN

HOOK (0-2s): 1986. Romania. Workers drilling for a power plant break into a chamber that had been sealed... for five and a half million years.
ESC1: The air inside is poison. Almost no oxygen. A hundred times the carbon dioxide. Total darkness.
TWIST: And yet — it's crawling with life. Blind spiders. White leeches. An eyeless scorpion. Thirty-seven species that exist nowhere else on Earth, feeding on bacteria that eat toxic gas instead of sunlight.
PAYOFF: That's why NASA studies this cave. Because if life can work down here... it can work on other worlds.
LOOP TAIL: Barely a hundred humans have ever been inside. The door stays locked.

FACTS (verified Oct 29, Wikipedia + spacedaily):
- Discovered 1986, Cristian Lascu, power-plant survey boreholes, Mangalia, Romania
- Sealed ~5.5M years; 1 artificial entrance; access by permit only (~100 people ever)
- Air: 7-10% O2 (vs 21%), 2-3.5% CO2 (~100x), methane + H2S
- 57 animal species, 37 endemic (Wikipedia); eyeless/pigmentless; Nepa anophthalma water scorpion
- Only known land ecosystem fully on chemosynthesis; Europa/Enceladus analogue
