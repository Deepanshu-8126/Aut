=== DESCRIPTION ===
Movile Cave, Romania. Sealed for 5.5 million years. Poison air, zero light — and 37 species that exist nowhere else on Earth.

Found by accident in 1986 during a power plant survey. Fewer than ~100 humans have ever been inside. NASA studies it because if life works down there, it could work on Europa.
