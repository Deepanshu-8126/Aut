TITLE: it survived 10 days in open space - meet the tardigrade 👽
DESC:
The tardigrade is a real microscopic animal that survived 10 days in the vacuum of space. In 2007 ESA strapped 3,000 of them to the outside of a rocket — no suit, no air. Back on Earth, one drop of water woke them up. Some even had babies. It survives being frozen for 30 years, boiling heat and 600x lethal radiation. And it's been on Earth 500 million years — living in the wet moss outside your front door.
