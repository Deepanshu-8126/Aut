# C12 — THE DARVAZA GAS CRATER ("GATES OF HELL") (Chronic) — METADATA

TITLE: the burning pit that has raged for over 50 years 🔥💀
SECONDARY TITLE: in 1971, soviets set a gas crater on fire... it never stopped 💀

DESC:
In 1971, Soviet geologists drilling in the Karakum Desert of Turkmenistan accidentally collapsed the ground into a massive natural gas cavern. Fearing toxic methane leaks, they lit it on fire — expecting it to burn out in days. 

Instead, the Darvaza Gas Crater ("The Gates of Hell") has roared with perpetual flames for over half a century.
