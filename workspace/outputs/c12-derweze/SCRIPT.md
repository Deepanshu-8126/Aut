In the middle of the Karakum Desert burns a pit that refuses to die.
The Darvaza Gas Crater. Locals call it... the Gates of Hell.
In 1971, Soviet geologists drilling for oil accidentally punctured a subterranean cavern.
The ground collapsed into a seventy-meter wide abyss, releasing toxic methane gas.
To protect local villages, geologists decided to burn it off.
They expected the fire to consume all the gas in a matter of days.
Instead, it ignited an inferno that has raged continuously for over half a century.
Gigantic flames roaring from thousands of underground vents.
Even today, scientists don't know how much gas remains trapped below.
