# VIDEO 65 — MILK ATTACKS 🥛 (Funcyous)

## TITLE
Why Milk Always Boils Over (But Water Never Does) 🥛🔥

## DESCRIPTION
Look away for ten seconds... and your milk attacks. 😤
But water? Water never escapes the pot.

Here's the secret: heat pushes milk's fat and proteins to the surface, where they weave a thin invisible skin. Steam bubbles rush up — but the skin won't let them pop. So they stack into an army of foam... and climb right over the edge.

Water has no skin. Its bubbles burst free. That's the whole trick.

And the wooden spoon hack? It really works — it breaks the skin.

Some clips are representative stock footage. Animation is illustrative.
