DESCRIPTION:
Mad honey from Nepal is real — hallucinogenic honey made by the world's largest bee on 300-foot Himalayan cliffs.
Its grayanotoxin comes from rhododendron nectar. Small doses make the room spin; large doses slow the heart. Around 65 BC, allies of King Mithridates left bowls of it out for Pompey's Roman soldiers — about 1,000 never got up (Strabo's account). Gurung hunters still harvest it from cliff faces today.
